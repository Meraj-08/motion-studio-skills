# Example prompts

## New film
- "Make a 30-second launch film for our app from these recordings (rec/*.mp4). Site: https://example.com. Study
  refs/notion.mp4 for pacing first and show me the shot list before you build."
- "Turn our export feature into a 20-second square launch clip. Frame-number the shots and show me three stills first."

## References
- "Here are three launch videos I love. What exactly do they do that ours doesn't? Measure it."
- "Analyse how this film uses sound second by second and tell me what to copy (the technique, not the music)."

## Music
- "Fit this track to the film: the drop on Send at 12.9 s, suspense when the checks fail, the biggest hit on Merged."
- "Slow the music a little but keep every hit on its frame."
- "Give me versions with these three licensed tracks."

## Sound effects and mix
- "The merge sound feels late and I can't hear the typing."
- "Add soft UI clicks, varied typing, and a whoosh into each scene."
- "The background is too loud; make the effects a bit louder. Master it for Instagram."
- "It sounds flat from 13 to 50 seconds."

## Picture
- "I rendered v3. Score it honestly and fix the three worst things."
- "The app screen looks flat. Show me backgrounds like on our website, as stills first."
- "Replace the hotspot pill in the phone recording with a normal Wi-Fi status bar."
- "Use version B before 20 seconds and version A after, with no visible join."
