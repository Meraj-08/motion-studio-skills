# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique work rather than people, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and sharing others' private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Meraj-08/motion-studio-skills/security/advisories/new) or a direct
message on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions
that break these rules, and may ban repeat offenders.
